# 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Sky Star is a new B2B trading and sourcing brand.

The brand is starting from zero:

- No existing logo
- No established visual identity
- No existing website
- No established design system
- No approved photography library
- No published client testimonials or case studies yet

The website therefore needs to establish credibility without inventing proof.

## Core positioning

> **One point of contact, from requirement to delivery.**

Sky Star helps international businesses source products from Asia, coordinate purchasing and arrange delivery to their destination.

## Primary website objective

Convert qualified visitors into genuine B2B sourcing enquiries.

## Secondary objectives

- Establish trust during buyer due diligence
- Explain the business model clearly
- Communicate the service offering without looking like a marketplace
- Support traffic from LinkedIn, WhatsApp, email, referrals and outbound sales
- Make it easy for international buyers to start a conversation

## Primary conversion

**Request a Quote**

## Secondary conversion

**Chat on WhatsApp**

# 2. IMPORTANT BUSINESS & CONTENT RULES

These rules are mandatory.

## Do not invent facts

Never invent:

- Client names
- Client logos
- Testimonials
- Awards
- Certifications
- Factory ownership
- Years of experience
- Employee numbers
- Customer statistics
- Order volumes
- Countries served
- Guaranteed prices
- Guaranteed delivery times
- Superlatives
- Unsupported partnerships
- Unsupported credentials

Any unresolved company information must remain clearly marked as `[TBC]` until supplied.

## Public product restrictions

Vape and atomizer products must **not** appear on the public website at launch.

They must remain omitted unless management and legal review explicitly approve an alternative treatment.

## DDP wording

DDP must always be presented as conditional.

Use wording such as:

> DDP arrangements may be available for eligible product categories and destinations. Availability depends on the product and applicable import requirements and is confirmed for each order.

Do not present DDP as an unrestricted global capability.

## Category strategy

Do not give every product category equal visual weight.

Primary/anchor categories:

- Hardware & Consumer Products
- LED Lighting
- Home Appliances

Supporting categories:

- Building Materials
- Furniture
- Mobile & Accessories
- Other Product Requirements

The site should feel like a capable sourcing company, not a general marketplace.

# 19. COLOUR DIRECTION

Use:

- Deep navy as the primary colour
- Warm or neutral off-white background
- Cool light grey
- Deep charcoal text
- One restrained accent colour

Suggested direction:

Primary navy: `#0E2A47`

Secondary navy: `#1B4470`

Accent starting point: `#E8842C`

Text: `#1C1F23`

Muted text: `#6B7280`

Light background: `#F4F6F8`

White: `#FFFFFF`

These are starting references, not mandatory final brand colours. The new Sky Star logo and visual identity should ultimately determine the final palette.

## Colour rules

- Keep the accent restrained
- No gradient-heavy design
- No neon colours
- No purple/pink AI aesthetic
- No text on insufficiently contrasting backgrounds
- Test all combinations to WCAG AA

---

# 20. TYPOGRAPHY

Use a modern, premium sans-serif system.

Desired characteristics:

- Highly legible
- Contemporary
- International
- Professional
- Strong editorial headlines

Possible type direction:

- Inter
- Inter Tight
- Söhne
- Neue Haas Grotesk
- Another comparable production-ready sans-serif

Do not use:

- Decorative serif display fonts
- Futuristic novelty fonts
- Multiple unrelated font families

Recommended hierarchy:

- H1: large editorial display
- H2: strong section heading
- H3: service/category heading
- Body: approximately 16–18px desktop
- Comfortable line-height
- Strong text measure

Use fluid typography with `clamp()` where appropriate.

---

# 21. LAYOUT SYSTEM

Use a disciplined responsive grid.

## Desktop

- 12-column grid
- Content max-width around 1280px
- Wider section container up to approximately 1440px
- 24px gutters where appropriate

## Tablet

- 8-column logic

## Mobile

- 4-column logic or equivalent single-column layout

Use consistent spacing tokens.

Avoid random margins and arbitrary positioning.

---

# 22. RESPONSIVE DESIGN — NON-NEGOTIABLE

The site must be deliberately designed for:

- 320px
- 375px
- 390px
- 430px
- 768px
- 834px
- 1024px
- 1280px
- 1440px
- 1920px+

Also test:

- Mobile portrait
- Mobile landscape
- Tablet portrait
- Tablet landscape
- Desktop
- Large desktop

## Responsive rules

1. Do not simply shrink the desktop layout.
2. Re-compose complex layouts for smaller screens.
3. No horizontal scrolling.
4. No clipped text.
5. No overlapping content.
6. No content dependent on fixed heights.
7. No important content hidden accidentally.
8. Images must crop and resize intelligently.
9. Forms become single-column on mobile.
10. Navigation becomes a proper mobile menu.
11. CTA remains easy to find.
12. Touch targets should be at least 44 × 44 CSS pixels.
13. Maintain readable text measure.
14. Reduce section spacing carefully on mobile.
15. Use mobile-specific hero image crops where needed.
16. Decorative content can be removed on smaller screens when necessary.

## Mobile header

- Compact sticky header
- Visible logo
- Accessible menu
- Request a Quote remains accessible
- Full-height or near-full-height menu treatment when opened
- Correct focus management
- Escape-to-close

# 24. ACCESSIBILITY — WCAG 2.2 AA TARGET

Build to WCAG 2.2 AA.

## Requirements

- Semantic HTML
- One clear H1
- Logical heading hierarchy
- Skip link
- Keyboard navigation
- Visible keyboard focus
- Accessible buttons
- Accessible accordion
- Accessible form labels
- Error messages linked to fields
- Do not use colour as the only error indicator
- Sufficient contrast
- Meaningful alt text
- Reduced motion support
- Logical tab order
- Accessible mobile navigation
- Comfortable touch targets

All interactive components must remain usable without a mouse.

# 25. MOTION

Motion should reinforce hierarchy and quality.

Allowed:

- Gentle fade + small translate
- Refined hover transitions
- Subtle image reveals
- Small nav transitions
- Sticky header transitions

Avoid:

- Scroll hijacking
- Heavy parallax
- Auto-playing visual loops
- Particle backgrounds
- Typewriter effects
- Flashy counters
- Excessive bouncing or scaling

Respect:

`prefers-reduced-motion`

# 31. PERFORMANCE

Performance is part of credibility.

Target:

- Lighthouse mobile 90+
- LCP under 2 seconds where realistically achievable
- INP under 200ms
- CLS under 0.1
- Total initial page weight kept lean
- Minimal third-party JavaScript

Use:

- AVIF/WebP
- Responsive `srcset`
- Lazy loading below the fold
- Explicit image dimensions
- Self-hosted fonts
- `font-display: swap`
- Deferred non-critical JS
- Minimal dependencies

Avoid:

- Heavy page builders
- Large animation libraries
- Unnecessary analytics scripts
- Floating live chat widgets
- AI chatbots at launch
